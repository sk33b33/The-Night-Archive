# 3D model credits

The furniture models in this folder are from the **Furniture Kit (2.0)** by
**Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)**:
free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects, with no
attribution required. Credited here with thanks.

They were converted from the kit's OBJ files to binary glTF by
`scripts/convert-models.mjs`. The kit itself is not stored in this
repository; download it from https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit to
reconvert or add models.

Everything else in the reading room (walls, the filing cabinet and its
drawers, folders, the Unfiled tray, the door, the clock and labels) is built
in code in `src/world/`.
