# Season 2 arc (DRAFT for review)

Status: **proposal**. Seven cases, 008–014, matching Season 1. Cases 008 and
009 are built and approved. Each later case gets its own bible for approval
before it is built.

## Recommendation: the reveal

**Reveal at the finale (Case 014), at level 2. Never explain how.**

- **Cases 010–013 keep reveal level 1.** The records say "M. —", and no
  identity link to the player is accepted.
- **Case 014:** a record finally names the night archivist at NA-07 as
  **"Mara —"** (surname still withheld). Supported finding: *the second
  voice on the 1981 reel and tonight's recording addresses the night
  archivist at NA-07 by first name.*
- **Never accepted, even in the finale:** how the 1981 voice knew; whether
  the 1979 "M." and the player are the same person; time travel or any
  mechanism.

**Why:** Season 1 earned its restraint, and seven cases of "M." need a
payoff. The payoff is a *name*, not an *explanation*. That keeps the long
mystery ("how") for Season 3 while giving the player something true and
earned. It follows the brief: atmosphere from precise details, never
conclusions beyond the evidence.
